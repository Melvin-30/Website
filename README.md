# 🎓 Std 12th IT Subject SOPs Portal (Maharashtra Board)

Welcome to the ultimate resource hub for **Maharashtra State Board 12th Standard** students appearing for their **Information Technology (Subject Code: 99)** exams. This portal is meticulously designed to provide students with the necessary materials, practical guidance, and a realistic exam environment to excel in their board year.

---

## 🌐 Live Website
**🔗 [12thITSOP](https://12thitsop.netlify.app/)**

---

## 🌟 Overview
The **Std 12th IT SOPs Portal** serves as a one-stop destination for students of both **Science** and **Commerce** streams. It simplifies the learning process for Skill Oriented Practicals (SOPs) and provides a state-of-the-art mock testing environment that mirrors the final board exam experience.

### 🎯 Objective
- To provide clear, accessible, and structured SOP solutions.
- To help students understand the conduct and format of the IT Mock/Final Board Exams.
- To provide a platform for continuous practice through dynamic testing.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📚 Comprehensive SOP Library
Access a wide range of practicals organized by stream:
- **Commerce Stream**: Advanced Web Designing, Digital Marketing, Computerised Accounting, and Database Concepts.
- **Science Stream**: Advanced Web Designing, Advanced JavaScript, and Server-Side Scripting (PHP).

### 📝 Dynamic Mock Test Engine
The standout feature of this portal is the **Mock Exam System**, designed to simulate the actual Board Exam environment:
- **Unique Paper Generation**: Unlike static tests, our engine generates **different question papers for every user/student** sessions. Each click of the "Generate Paper" button picks a random set of questions from our extensive question bank.
- **Realistic Exam UI**: Features a seat number entry (mock), countdown timer (2h 30m), and section-wise navigation.
- **Instant Result & Scoring**: Students can "Finish Exam" to see their score instantly.
- **Answer Key Reveal**: A dedicated "Show Answers" feature to help students learn from their mistakes.

### 📂 Additional Resources
- **Textbooks**: Direct access to IT board textbooks.
- **Useful Links**: Curated tutorials from W3Schools for HTML, CSS, JS, and PHP.

## 📖 How to Use
1. **Explore SOPs**: Scroll through the main page or use the navigation bar to find the SOP you want to study.
2. **Take a Mock Test**: Navigate to the "Mock Test" dropdown and select your stream.
3. **Generate Paper**: Click the **Generate Paper** button to receive your unique set of questions.
4. **Practice & Review**: Complete the exam and review your answers to improve.
